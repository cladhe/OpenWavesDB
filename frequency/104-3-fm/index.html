Data source: ARCOM's (arcom.fr) public bulk export of all French FM/DAB+ transmitters, dated Jul 22 2026. This is the authoritative source for each station's real registered name, regulatory category and full nationwide frequency list, but it has no city field. City-specific assignments come from per-city directories (onde-radio.fr for most regions, plus paris-friendly.fr and radioscope.fr for Paris), and every single one is individually cross-checked against ARCOM's own frequency list for that exact station name before being included. Any frequency ARCOM doesn't confirm is left out rather than guessed, which is why a city's count here can run a little lower than what a directory site claims. A few stations are still filed under a legacy registered name that differs from their real on-air brand (Virgin Radio, for instance, is nationally registered as "Europe 2", its pre-2011 name); the current on-air name is shown here instead. This reflects regulatory authorizations, not confirmed live broadcast hours, which aren't published anywhere; all entries are modeled as continuous daily broadcasts, the norm for licensed FM stations. More French cities are being added over time, so check back and use the region picker next to "France". Mexico's entries come from IFT's official Infraestructura de Estaciones FM register (ift.org.mx infraestructura-de-estaciones), pulled directly with no city-level cross-checking needed since IFT's own register already includes it. Use the region picker next to "Mexico" to narrow it down to a major city, or leave it on "All Mexico". USA entries come from the FCC Media Bureau's FM Query database, filtered to Full Service FM and Licensed Stations only (no translators, LPFM, or boosters), snapshot dated Jul 31 2026. Use the region picker next to "United States" to narrow it down to a state or territory, or leave it on "All USA". United Kingdom entries come from Ofcom's open TxParams dataset, which unlike the USA's FCC data includes each station's real on-air brand name. Canada entries come from ISED's SMS Authorization Data Extract bulk data, operational stations only, filtered down from a North America-wide coordination database that also lists USA and Mexico border-zone stations. Brazil entries come from ANATEL's Spectrum-E public channel database, filtered to licensed channels only; ANATEL's data has no brand-name or call-sign field, so the licensee's legal company name is shown instead. Australia entries come from the ACMA's Register of Radiocommunications Licences bulk extract, filtered to National/Commercial/Community FM broadcast subservices only.
