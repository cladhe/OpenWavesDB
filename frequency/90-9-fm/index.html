Data source: ARCOM's (arcom.fr) public bulk export of all French FM/DAB+ transmitters, dated Jul 22 2026. This is the authoritative source for each station's real registered name, regulatory category and full nationwide frequency list, but it has no city field. City-specific assignments come from per-city directories (onde-radio.fr for most regions, plus paris-friendly.fr and radioscope.fr for Paris), and every single one is individually cross-checked against ARCOM's own frequency list for that exact station name before being included. Any frequency ARCOM doesn't confirm is left out rather than guessed, which is why a city's count here can run a little lower than what a directory site claims. A few stations are still filed under a legacy registered name that differs from their real on-air brand (Virgin Radio, for instance, is nationally registered as "Europe 2", its pre-2011 name); the current on-air name is shown here instead. This reflects regulatory authorizations, not confirmed live broadcast hours, which aren't published anywhere; all entries are modeled as continuous daily broadcasts, the norm for licensed FM stations.
