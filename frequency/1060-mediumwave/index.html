Data sources: FCC AM Query (fcc.gov/media/radio/am-query), licensed stations only; ISED's Broadcasting Database (amstatio.dbf), operational stations only; Ofcom's TxParams MF register (ofcom.org.uk radio-tech-parameters), every registered UK MW transmitter; IFT's Infraestructura de Estaciones AM register (ift.org.mx infraestructura-de-estaciones), every registered Mexican AM concesión/permiso; ACMA's Broadcast Transmitter Excel register (acma.gov.au list-transmitters-licence-broadcast), every registered Australian AM transmitter (main/standby duplicates at a site collapsed to one row); and for Germany, no regulator export exists at all (the Bundesnetzagentur doesn't publish MW as a bulk dataset), so those 5 entries were individually cross-checked across multiple independent radio-hobbyist and museum sources instead. Everything was pulled in Jul 2026. This reflects each source's engineering authorizations or self-reported schedule, not live on-air status at any given moment: a small number of stations may be temporarily silent, and Germany's part-time entries in particular only transmit on the schedule noted in their Days/Info column. Programming language and format aren't tracked by the regulators, so they aren't guessed here either. "Transmitter / Country" shows city, state/province, or transmitter site/area instead, since every row already belongs to one of these six countries (US state and Canadian province codes never collide, so "ON" is always Ontario, for instance).
