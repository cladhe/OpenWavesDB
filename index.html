Data comes from EiBi's "BC A26" shortwave schedule (valid Mar 29 – Oct 25 2026, last updated Jul 21 2026): eibispace.de. It's free to copy and distribute under the source's own terms. This table covers all 14 shortwave broadcast bands but leaves out non-broadcast traffic (utility, military, marine, aeronautical); for the complete, continuously updated global listing including that traffic, download the full frequency file directly from EiBi, or check shortwavedb.org, which aggregates EiBi, AOKI and HFCC. "Transmitter / Country" reflects the originating country as listed in the source; EiBi's per-site codes need its separate site-code table, which isn't included here. Frequencies are the station's nominal or licensed channel. Real transmitters can drift a little off that (a few are logged with a decimal, like 7280.1 kHz), so treat these as a starting point and expect to fine-tune once you're actually listening.
